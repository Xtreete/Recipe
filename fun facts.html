🐙 Fact 1: The Amazing Octupus

octupuses have threee hearts, nine brains, and blue blood. Two hearts pump blood to the grills, while the third pumps it to the rest of the body.

How smart are octupuses?

Octupuses can open jars, solve puzzles, and even recognise human faces. Scientists consider them one of the most intellegent invertebrates on earth.
