How to make tasty Butter Chicken😋 by anvika

Ingerdients🛒

- 250 gramm chicken
- 50 to 100 gramm butter
- 1 medium sized onion
- 1 tomato
- 100 gramm cashew nuts
- 2 clove from a garlic
- 1 ginger
- Dried fenugreek leaves
- Chilli powder
- Turmeric powder
- Garam masala powder

preparing the dish

Firstly, cut the chicken into small pieces and marinate them with turmeric, chilli powder, curd and lemon.
Keep the chicken aside for 30 minutes
After that, you want to pan fry the chicken for 12 minutes at medium heat.
Then, leave the chicken aside and turn off the stove.
In a pan, put a slice of butter on medium heat. While the butter is melting, chop the onions into cubes,
the tomato and cloves into fine pieces, and cut out a small fraction of the ginger.
When finished, throw all of the vegetables onto the pan
bye!!
